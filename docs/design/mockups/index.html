Three design directions

Each shows the same two screens (labelling a reply, and how far to trust the judge) with the same real numbers. Try each in light and dark, and at phone width.

  • A. Notebook: calm, one column, results as sentences.
  • B. Instrument: dense, everything on one screen, criteria compared in a table.
  • C. Guided: one step at a time, step rail, result cards.
