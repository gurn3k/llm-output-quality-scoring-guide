# Sofa So Good: customer support policy

Sofa So Good is a fictional online shop that sells kitchenware, lamps and home textiles. This policy is made up for testing and describes no real company.

## Returns
- Items can be returned within **30 days of delivery** if unused and in their original packaging.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within **5-7 business days** after the return arrives at our warehouse.
- **Clearance items are final sale** and can't be returned or exchanged, unless they arrive damaged or defective.
- Return shipping is **free when the item is damaged, defective or not what was ordered**. For any other return, a **$6.95** prepaid label is deducted from the refund.

## Damaged or defective items
- Customers should report damage or defects **within 14 days of delivery**, with a photo.
- We send a replacement or a refund, whichever the customer prefers.
- For damaged items **under $25**, the customer keeps or recycles the item; no return is needed.

## Shipping
- Standard shipping takes **3-7 business days**. Express takes **1-2 business days**.
- Agents may give these estimates, but **must never guarantee a delivery date**.

## Order changes
- Orders can be cancelled or changed (address, items) **within 1 hour** of being placed. After that, the order ships as placed, and the customer can return it once it arrives.

## Price adjustments
- If an item's price drops **within 7 days of purchase**, we refund the difference **once per item**. Clearance items aren't eligible.

## Goodwill
- If a delivery is more than **5 business days later than its estimate**, agents may offer a **one-time 10% discount code** for a future order.
- Agents may not offer any other discount, credit, free item or fee waiver.

## Warranty
- Electrical items (kettles, toasters, lamps) carry a **1-year warranty** against manufacturing defects from the date of delivery.

## When to escalate
Agents must hand these to a specialist and must not try to resolve them alone. The reply acknowledges the issue, says a specialist will reply **within 1 business day**, and gives any immediate safety advice.
- An injury or safety hazard: burns, fire, smoke, sparks, electric shock.
- Legal action: the customer mentions a lawyer, a lawsuit, or a complaint to a regulator.
- A chargeback or payment dispute with the customer's bank.
- Suspected fraud or account compromise: orders the customer didn't place, or a changed password or email they didn't change.
- A lost order worth more than **$500**.
- A customer who has asked for a manager **twice** in the conversation.

## Tone and privacy
- Be polite, plain and specific. Don't blame the customer.
- Never ask for a full card number or a password.
